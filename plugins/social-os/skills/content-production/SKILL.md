# Content production

Retrieve current context and relevant prior content. Identify audience need, core promise, objective, differentiation, production feasibility and the intended evidence of success. Avoid generic viral claims. Fix an unclear idea before expanding its production requirements.

For factual material, research authoritative evidence and maintain claim records with actual URLs, excerpts, dates and supported/disputed/unverified status. Do not mark a claim supported without the source having been inspected. Preserve genuine source disagreement.

Draft a script or copy that fulfills its hook. Match voice and audience understanding. Use narrative structures appropriate to the piece rather than one universal template. Include a useful CTA and packaging options when relevant.

Save the master content through content_save. The tool accepts a complete content object; retrieve and preserve existing fields on edits. Master text changes increment master_revision. Do not mark old variants as current until they have been reviewed against the changed master.

For a small request, do not insist on a full workflow. For a production package, save checkpoints, adapt native variants, run qc_run and use content_export. Clearly distinguish a draft from structural readiness and from human editorial acceptance.
