# Channel creation and growth

For a new channel, do not invent a niche before the niche-selection/research step is complete. Once selected, propose channel names in a compact numbered table with positioning, memorability/search relevance and brand potential. Accept custom names.

After selection, treat the chosen channel identity as canonical project context. Develop audience, positioning, description/handle ideas and content pillars without asking for information already known.

When logo or banner assets are needed, ask one compact asset decision: Generate both; Logo only; Banner only; Prompts only; Use existing. If generation is selected and host image generation is available, create the requested assets. Keep each generated asset associated with its channel/asset role in the working project context. Do not claim an asset was generated if only a prompt was created.
