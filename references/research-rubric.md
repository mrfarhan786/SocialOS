# SocialOS research rubric v2.1

Use fresh evidence for time-sensitive recommendations. Prefer selected connected specialist tools when the user has approved their use, then primary/official sources and authoritative reporting/data. Community signals may supplement stronger evidence.

For topic discovery, inspect recent signals with emphasis on the last 10 days where available and relevant examples of unusually strong channel/content growth over roughly the last 1–3 months. Do not claim that a channel is newly created, rapidly growing, or highly profitable unless the evidence actually establishes that fact.

Opportunity fields may include:
- Recency and source/tool signal.
- Growth/trend evidence.
- Reach potential.
- Engagement potential.
- Viral potential.
- Competition.
- Monetization potential.
- Longevity.
- Fit with channel production type, video type, duration and style.

Use qualitative assessments when credible numeric data is unavailable. Never invent earnings, revenue, RPM/CPM, subscriber growth, search volume, trend score or forecast. Views do not prove earnings. Distinguish observed evidence from SocialOS-created opportunity ideas.
